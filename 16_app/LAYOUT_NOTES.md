## Rules distilled (apply to every hold composition)
1. ONE idea per frame. Drop the extra small line / corner text / "A LIVING FORM" add-ons (L1b, L3b, L4a, M1b, M2a, M3a, M5a, N6, P1, P7, P11, S12).
2. Massive type that fills or is cropped by the frame (M2a, P1 "DG", L1b filled D, P7 forms).
3. Stacked lines in contrasting weights/faces, justified (N3, N1 stacked top-right quadrant, N5 spine, N8 hung from top).
4. Push text to edges/corners; justified into one quadrant, scaled to the full width of that quadrant (R1 edges + junction word, R2 bottom-right).
5. Type on SOLID flat contrasting strips / blocks (Q4, Q7, Q9, Q12, Q6 angled strips). Slightly-not-straight strips contrast nicely (S8). Blocky contrasted split sides (S1, liked).
6. Words inside letter counters with colour (P4 "great — strong simple visual").
7. Circle of type (M5a), construction triangle (M3b "good"), letters in cells (P9).
8. Shapes + text: keep SIMPLE (S9, S10 too complicated); keep text together, not scattered (S2).
9. Legibility: never stretch/squash so words go illegible (Q2, Q5, S1 vertical); small contrast text must still be readable (M1b, N7).
10. The layout page is for COMPOSITION ONLY (user 2026-10-26). Ignore the faces on the plates; every font comes from
    the user's favourites (`mograph/fonts/liked.json`, fonts artifact VRGNVX5U78uiRL5DJtWDpb).
