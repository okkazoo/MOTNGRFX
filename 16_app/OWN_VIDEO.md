## What worked
- The whole route ran on the existing nodes at the first try: footage = the user's frames, music = the user's audio, Gemma 31B designed the type, 6 words each form in a shot, land centred, hold ~1.5 s, exit before the cut; 3D cuts designed by Gemma (depth_pivot after s2, orbit:push after s4) rendered; hard cuts elsewhere.

## What the redesign path could not do (gaps for the app version)
1. Spans come from the plan, not from the video: the video's own cuts (0.3, 1.33, 2.03, 4.2, 4.7, 8.0, 10.03, 10.7, 12.7, 16.0, 17.33, 18.7, 21.4, 22.07 s; ffmpeg scene detection = `25_music-read/v001/cuts.json`) fall INSIDE the planned shots (only ~3 coincide with a planned downbeat cut). Needed: a request key `cuts` (explicit spans) honoured by `cut_spans`, or segments = the video's cuts merged on bars, with a per-segment matte.
2. One Alpha-Gen matte per planned shot is not cut-aware: in a shot that contains a b-roll insert (train, street, graffiti) the matte keeps a PHANTOM person (`review/mattes.png`: the train and street frames carry a head-and-shoulders matte). Fix: a matte per video cut (sub-clip) concatenated into the shot's matte, or a per-frame emptiness gate.
3. Behind-the-subject did not show: the design chose the `behind` technique and the default chain has `sandwich`, but in the sampled holds (FIRST WORD over the face at 3 s) the type sits IN FRONT of the head (legibility guard / knockout over the matte, plus the phantom mattes). No word was clearly occluded by the man in this run. Needs a check of `legible` / cover after the cut-aware matte (gap 2) and a design rule that one word is `subject_through` with a visible occlusion held for a beat.
4. Letters-as-window on a plain ground was not produced (the compose cuts are hard / depth_pivot / orbit only; window_other / window_cut need another shot as the window and the guide did not pick them). The request has no field for it.
5. The redesign request cannot carry a design direction: `MographArchiveLoad.request_from` copies only words / scene / music / length / look / seed / quality / format / shots and drops `idea` / `owners`, so "centred, on the beat, hold >= 1 beat, one word behind, one window" went in through the `music` text (the prompt then says "generated instrumental music from the prompt" and "lyrics: none (instrumental)": wrong for a sung track). Needed: a real `direction` input and a lyrics line (the reading's `prominent_words` with landing beats as locked hits).
6. Landing times are the planner's (hits moved into the shot: 5.31 / 7.98 / 13.31 ...), not the music reading's (1.31 / 5.31 / 9.31 / 12.65 / 17.31 / 21.31): the plan schedules one lockup per shot from the shot start. Holds are ~1.5 s (about 2 beats), set by the guide's floor, not asked from the reading.
7. Centring and stillness are not enforced for this route: dy offsets -0.2..0.2 and yaw +-10.5 deg (3D perspective) were kept; BASS HIT sits low on the body.
8. Little variety: one module (`staircase`) for all six words, 3 liked fonts, 2 io styles (the piece rule <= 2 io styles flattened the design); extruded white type with glow on every word.
9. Output is 540x960 draft only; a final-quality route needs the clip at 1080x1920 and the matte at 1088x1920 (Alpha-Gen is single-stage 576x1024 here).
10. Audio is AAC re-encoded by MographAssemble (corr 0.99996), not bit-exact; a bit-exact route would mux the source audio stream after the assemble (`ffmpeg -c:a copy`).
11. 30 -> 24 fps resample (frame drops): the pipeline and LTX are fixed at 24 fps.

## The finish step of overlay: output = the SOURCE frame outside the type
`overlay_mode.restore_video` (box, after the render): for every frame, E = the composed frame, R = the engine's own footage for that frame (the first `span` frames of each `sK_clip.mp4`), S = the window clip (the user's pixels). Mask = `|E - R|` (max over channels, 3x3 median to kill codec noise) > 20, grown 4 px, feathered 1.5 px; output = S outside, E inside. Written at the source rate (`-r`), libx264 crf 14, then muxed with the audio (stream copy for the clip's own AAC). A codec-noisy render cannot alter the picture; `restore_frame` is unit-tested bit-exact outside the type. Limits: an anti-aliased type edge fainter than 20 levels drops out; the ink inside the grown mask comes from the render.

(A track's lyrics are the page's READ, not a new Whisper run inside the job.)

`python tools/app/overlay_check.py SOURCE.mp4 .../own/<job>/final.mp4 --report .../own/<job>/overlay_report.json` (exit 0 = pass). It compares size, fps (exact rational), frame count / duration, per-frame mean abs diff and PSNR outside the type (word windows from the report, or `--mask-video`), the type's share of the frame, and that the source's hard cuts sit on the same frame numbers. `tests/test_overlay_mode.py` runs the same function on synthetic clips (pass; a graded copy fails; a short copy fails).

## Risks (not measured)
- Compose / type / flow at the source size has only run at the tier sizes before (360x640, 540x960; 1080x1920 compose existed for `final` but was never rendered by this route). Above 1080p (1440p, 4K) VRAM and time grow with pixels (4K = 4x 1080p in the type passes, the numpy compose workers and the full-frame restore). The matte stays 1024 long, so only compose / restore scale.
- Square and other non-9:16 / 16:9 sources run with `format` = the nearer orientation and the layouts fitted to the real aspect; untested.
- The redesign graph's 3D cuts at the source size are untested.
- The lossless window clip needs disk (about 1.5 GB for 30 s of 1080p30).

## Readable behind + colour from the shot (2026-11-02, user review of an own-video overlay job: "most of the text neither fully over nor fully behind", "why that light peach", "what happened to pulling colours from the shot")
Root causes found on the real job (box job 20261101-160830-aec4, frame 406, a man standing centre frame, a two-line word behind him):
1. The design drew the type with `sandwich(matte = luminosity_key(footage, 0.18 .. 0.55))`: the "subject" was whatever is bright. The black leather jacket (luma < 0.18) was transparent, so letter fragments showed through the body in patches, and a luminosity key is not a matte, so `overlay_mode.apply_behind` and `recipe_eval.guard_ops` (both read a matte-like alpha) never touched the word (`overlay_behind.chains: 0` in the retime report). The Alpha-Gen mattes themselves were solid (cached, not touched).
2. The behind choice was a quota (about half of the words, relaxed floor: the subject may hide 50 % of the word, any letter down to 10 %), chosen on the word's box, not on its letters.
3. Colour: shot hues were taken from the whole frame, the person's skin included (first hue 31 deg on 3 of 5 words); the contrast test was a luminosity histogram of a wide band that no one ink reaches 85 % of, so 4 of 5 words fell through to the whole-clip k-means palette (a brown (98, 56, 24) = skin + leather + corner wood) whose lightness was raised until it read: peach (220, 165, 128), (244, 187, 149), (255, 235, 231).
Fixes (both repos): `tools/app/overlay_mode.py` `solid_occluder` (every luminosity-key occluder gets `upscale_matte($matte) -> fill_holes(close)` next to it, post-processing at compositing time on the cached mattes; no Alpha-Gen change), `VIS_RULE` / `BEHIND_FLOOR` / `FRONT_FLOOR` (a word is BEHIND only while every glyph keeps >= 70 % and the word >= 85 % visible in all but 25 % of its hold frames, else FRONT whole; a front word is cleared over its whole span by the full type alpha), `glyph_stack` / `word_visibility` / `rule_verdict` / `plan_word` (the rule on the real letter shapes at design time, nudge <= 0.2 of the frame / 8 % sideways / 10 % smaller only where it passes with the subject still overlapping, else front), `choose_behind` (never picks a failing word to fill a quota); `compose.LEG_FLOOR` / `LEG_BEHIND` use the same numbers in every mode; `recipe_eval.guard_ops` treats a luminosity-key alpha like a matte; `tools/fusion/legible_color.py` `shot_hues(valid=)`, `prefer_hues`, `pick_ink` ladder (4.5 / 3.0 over 85 % / 70 % of the pixels the word sits on, min chroma 0.075, no peach ink, variety against the last two inks, the subject's own hue last), `own_video_v2_retime_box.py` decides front / behind before the colours and picks colours from the word's own box without the subject's pixels. Retime report: `readable_behind`, `overlay_behind.solid_occluder`, `colors.words[*]` (pick, rung, hues, skin_or_subject_hues, measured_on).
Needs a ComfyUI restart (not done: a batch was running): the new compose constants (`LEG_FLOOR`, `LEG_BEHIND`) and the guard_ops change in the ComfyUI process; the own-video overlay route does not depend on them (its legible ops carry explicit floors).
Tests: `tests/test_behind_colour_rules.py` (18), `tests/test_overlay_mode.py`, `tests/test_legibility_floor.py`, `tests/test_legibility_guard.py` (thresholds rewritten to the new rule).

## Format: Original | Half scale (2026-11-03, user: "a different format option when own video is chosen where it defaults to the original resolution and also the ability to do half scale for smaller renders")
**NOT proven on a GPU render.** Code, unit tests and a page check ran locally; no job has run with `scale: "half"` on a box.
- **Page (`web_app/app.js`, `paintFormat` / `vidSizes`):** with a video added the Format select shows two options, "Original (W×H)" (default) and "Half scale (w×h)", from the clip's display size (the browser's `videoWidth/Height`, rotation applied), each side floored to even, half = that / 2 floored to even (1081x1921 -> 1080x1920 / 540x960; 1090x1922 -> 544x960 half; 270x480 -> 134x240). The old "(video is 9:16 only)" greying is gone. Leaving video mode puts the normal 9:16 / 16:9 options and value back. `S.values.format` stays a valid aspect string = the clip's orientation ("16:9" when wider than tall, else "9:16"), so the story request / signature, `buildMediaJson` and `probeDims` never see the scale and Original <-> Half re-reads nothing and keeps the story (checked: no ComfyUI prompt posted). The board's frame ratio / `--fa` come from the clip's real size (`fitBoard`), not the dropdown. The choice is sent as `scale: "full" | "half"` in the POST body.
- **Route (`own_video_route.py`):** `scale` defaults to full; anything but "full" / "half" is a 400 (`scale: one of full, half`); `format` now accepts "9:16" | "16:9" (the clip's orientation, ignored by the source-size runner); passed as `--scale`, recorded in `request.json`.
- **Runner (`own_video_job.py --scale full|half`, `overlay_mode.scaled_size` / `native_sizes(..., scale)`):** the window clip stays at the FULL source size and fps. Half changes only `compose` / `shot` / `clip` / the type canvas (`type_canvas(half)`: multiples of 64 at or above the half size, aspect kept, compose cover-fits it back and crops the sliver; 1920x1080 half = 960x540 typed at 1024x576; 270x480 typed at 576x1024) and the final (`final.mp4` = half size, same fps, same frame count, same edit, audio untouched). For the overlay edit the "source picture" that `restore_video` keeps outside the type is `work/window_half.mp4`, the window clip scaled with the same lanczos filter as the shot clips (lossless). Log lines: `scale half: output size WxH (type canvas ...)`, the final's `final.mp4: ... (expected N frames, ... WxH, scale half)` and a WARNING if the final's size differs. `job.state params` and `info` carry `scale`; `overlay_report.json` has `scale`, `size` (output) and `source_size`.
- **Mattes are shared between Original and Half.** `matte_key` = the Alpha-Gen input clip's content + the Alpha-Gen graph. The matte size is computed from the FULL source (`native_sizes(...)["matte"]`), the Alpha-Gen input clips are cut from the full window clip at that size by commands that do not mention the scale, so the graph and (if x264 re-encodes the clip bit-identically, which is expected but unmeasured) the clip bytes are the same: a half job restores the mattes a full job stored and the other way round. Compose resamples the matte to the output size with `upscale_matte` (area filter when it is smaller than the matte). Keys that follow the scale by content: cut (script text), archive (shot clip bytes), design / parse / render (via the archive key), retime (argv `--native-size`); the global analysis clip is sized from the source so colour / busyness analysis is the same. The Gemma design call is therefore re-run for a half job (not shared with a full job).
- **`overlay_check.py`:** reads `scale` from the job's report (or `--scale half`) and expects the final at half the source size; the source frames are scaled to that size (lanczos) before the picture / cut checks.
- **Tests / checks:** `tests/test_own_video_scale.py` (39: route validation, even rounding incl. odd sources, matte size / key unchanged by scale, the cut script's matte-clip lines identical); `16_app/app_check/video_scale_check.mjs` (17 page checks against a running dev proxy). `tests/test_own_video_route.py` no longer expects 16:9 to be rejected (the page sends the clip's orientation).
- **Unproven until a box run:** a real half render (compose / type at sizes below the matte, e.g. 270x480 with a 576x1024 matte), that the matte cache hit happens across a full and a half job (x264 determinism), the `overlay_check` PASS at half size, and the type quality at half.
