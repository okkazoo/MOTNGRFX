## Steps and timings (RTX 4090 box, queue shared with another job)
1. Analysis: the 24 s re-run (`25_music-read/v001/work/your_clip_analysis.json`: 90 bpm, D# minor, downbeats 1.98 + k*2.667 s). CPU, seconds.
2. Shots: the planner decides the spans, not the video: `plan_rules.cut_spans(576, grid)` = [111, 64, 129, 63, 128, 81] frames (cuts on downbeats 4.625 / 7.29 / 12.67 / 15.29 / 20.625 s). `own_video.py plan` asks cut_spans itself and cuts the clip to exactly those spans, because MographPiecePlan re-plans from request + music and the clip frames must match its layout. ffmpeg on the box: resample 30 -> 24 fps (pipeline FPS), scale 540x960 -> 576x1024 (draft shot size = Alpha-Gen size), one mp4 per shot, each carrying 8k+1 frames (span + the real frames that follow, like an LTX shot longer than its span). A few seconds.
3. Alpha-Gen matte per shot (`ltx_ic.build("alpha")`, 576x1024, PNG frames, crop disabled): 93 / 39 / 77 / 38 / 78 / 46 s = 371 s for the six jobs. No depth job (the engine estimates depth itself; the pivot node got no depth inputs).
4. Words: one Gemma 4 31B call (MographGGUFVL, text only, json_mode, 30 s incl. load): 6 lockups, one per shot, from the Whisper lyrics + beat list: FIRST WORD / SECOND WORD / MIDDLE WORD / THIRD WORD / FOURTH WORD / LAST WORD (all from the lyrics; a subset of the music-read worker's 8 prominent words, which arrived after this call; the planner wants one lockup per shot = 6). Reply: `work/words_reply_raw.txt`.
5. Archive on the box (`output/own_video/arch_v001`: still.png, sK_clip.mp4, sK_matte/NNNNN.png, piece.json = rules plan of the request with quality draft, index.json). Seconds.
6. Redesign graph = `fusion_redesign.json` converted to the `gemma31b_qat` arm by `llm_arms.convert` + `build_graphs.insert_pivot_cut` (both imported read-only; graph file `work/redesign_gemma31b_pivot.json`, run copy `redesign_run1.json`). source_video = the original mp4 (its audio = the song). 589 s wall: ~310 s until the first compose frame (load, plan, RAFT flow, design package, Gemma 31B design call, parse, type passes) and ~280 s compose + pivot cuts + assemble + archive (coarse, read off the progress log; no per-node timing run). Peak VRAM 21.8 GiB. The design reply parsed with no repair: 70.9 % of 134 fields from the model, 39 mended by piece rules (hits moved into their shot, stacked lines for the 9 % cap-height floor, <= 2 io styles), 0 from the rules.

## What worked
- The whole route ran on the existing nodes at the first try: footage = the user's frames, music = the user's audio, Gemma 31B designed the type, 6 words each form in a shot, land centred, hold ~1.5 s, exit before the cut; 3D cuts designed by Gemma (depth_pivot after s2, orbit:push after s4) rendered; hard cuts elsewhere.
- Words are legible, big, mostly centred; stacked in two lines (WORD A1 / A2, WORD B1 / B2).

## What the redesign path could not do (gaps for the app version)
1. Spans come from the plan, not from the video: the video's own cuts (0.3, 1.33, 2.03, 4.2, 4.7, 8.0, 10.03, 10.7, 12.7, 16.0, 17.33, 18.7, 21.4, 22.07 s; ffmpeg scene detection = `25_music-read/v001/cuts.json`) fall INSIDE the planned shots (only ~3 coincide with a planned downbeat cut). Needed: a request key `cuts` (explicit spans) honoured by `cut_spans`, or segments = the video's cuts merged on bars, with a per-segment matte.
2. One Alpha-Gen matte per planned shot is not cut-aware: in a shot that contains a b-roll insert (train, street, graffiti) the matte keeps a PHANTOM person (`review/mattes.png`: the train and street frames carry a head-and-shoulders matte). Fix: a matte per video cut (sub-clip) concatenated into the shot's matte, or a per-frame emptiness gate.
3. Behind-the-subject did not show: the design chose the `behind` technique and the default chain has `sandwich`, but in the sampled holds (FIRST WORD over the face at 3 s) the type sits IN FRONT of the head (legibility guard / knockout over the matte, plus the phantom mattes). No word was clearly occluded by the man in this run. Needs a check of `legible` / cover after the cut-aware matte (gap 2) and a design rule that one word is `subject_through` with a visible occlusion held for a beat.
4. Letters-as-window on a plain ground was not produced (the compose cuts are hard / depth_pivot / orbit only; window_other / window_cut need another shot as the window and the guide did not pick them). The request has no field for it.
5. The redesign request cannot carry a design direction: `MographArchiveLoad.request_from` copies only words / scene / music / length / look / seed / quality / format / shots and drops `idea` / `owners`, so "centred, on the beat, hold >= 1 beat, one word behind, one window" went in through the `music` text (the prompt then says "generated instrumental music from the prompt" and "lyrics: none (instrumental)": wrong for a sung track). Needed: a real `direction` input and a lyrics line (the reading's `prominent_words` with landing beats as locked hits).
6. Landing times are the planner's (hits moved into the shot: 5.31 / 7.98 / 13.31 ...), not the music reading's (1.31 / 5.31 / 9.31 / 12.65 / 17.31 / 21.31): the plan schedules one lockup per shot from the shot start. Holds are ~1.5 s (about 2 beats), set by the guide's floor, not asked from the reading.
7. Centring and stillness are not enforced for this route: dy offsets -0.2..0.2 and yaw +-10.5 deg (3D perspective) were kept; MIDDLE WORD sits low on the body.
8. Little variety: one module (`staircase`) for all six words, 3 liked fonts, 2 io styles (the piece rule <= 2 io styles flattened the design); extruded white type with glow on every word.
9. Output is 540x960 draft only; a final-quality route needs the clip at 1080x1920 and the matte at 1088x1920 (Alpha-Gen is single-stage 576x1024 here).
10. Audio is AAC re-encoded by MographAssemble (corr 0.99996), not bit-exact; a bit-exact route would mux the source audio stream after the assemble (`ffmpeg -c:a copy`).
11. 30 -> 24 fps resample (frame drops): the pipeline and LTX are fixed at 24 fps.

## Edit mode: overlay only (default) / with transitions

Overlay only (default) puts the type over the submitted video and leaves everything else alone. With transitions allows the full design (transitions, grade, scene changes).

Overlay keeps:
- the source size, fps (exact rational) and frame count;
- the source's hard cuts, on the same frame numbers;
- every pixel outside the type: output = the source frame outside the letters.

Words can sit in front of the subject or behind it (the subject matte is cut from the same clip and applied only to that clip).

Timing: with lyrics available, each word lands on the second it is sung, snapped forward to the nearest beat (never before it is sung); a word not found keeps its beat slot. Without lyrics, words land on the beat grid.

Audio swap: the output can carry a different track in place of the source audio; the picture timing is unchanged.

Check a result (exit 0 = pass):

`python tools/app/overlay_check.py SOURCE.mp4 OUTPUT/final.mp4 --report OUTPUT/overlay_report.json`

It compares size, fps, frame count and duration, per-frame mean abs diff and PSNR outside the type, the type's share of the frame, and that the source cuts sit on the same frames. `tests/test_overlay_mode.py` runs the same check on synthetic clips.
