## 4. Independence composite (draft, CPU on the box, `24_clean-plate/scripts/composite_layers.py`; ffmpeg libx264, 4-7 s per clip)
Layers: plate (576x1024 output resized to 360x640) / word (flat white "DRIFT" / "FIELD", Anybody - a liked font - full width at mid height) / subject = clean clip x matte (premultiplied). Motion: the plate scales 1.12 and pans +3.5 % -> -3.5 % of the width; the subject drifts +8 % of the width sideways and scales 1.0 -> 1.10 about its bottom-centre; word fixed.
Order plate -> word -> subject, so the subject occludes the word (clearly visible: the kayak covers letters of DRIFT).
Outputs in `24_clean-plate/v001/out/`: `motion-graphic_clean-plate_v001_{k,w}_s1_comp_independent.mp4` (the composite), `..._static.mp4` (3 panels: original clip | clean plate | plate + cut-out subject at the original position = does the cut-out fit back), `..._layers.mp4` (plate | word layer | subject on grey); raw plates `..._{k,w}_s1_{384,576}_video_00001_.mp4` + PNG sequences. Notes: the word in the "layers" panel is the white text on dark; the subject has no shadow or reflection of its own once moved (the plate has none either: the LoRA removed them), so a moved subject floats; add a contact shadow in the compose if it matters.

## 5. What an app "plate + subject layers" route needs (HAND-OFF; nothing here edits the app graphs)
1. **Card / box:** `ltx-2.5-22b-ic-lora-clean-plate-1.0.safetensors` row (NOT added to the plain 24 GB card `motion-graphic` or the 16 GB / 3060 cards: add it there if wanted, 0.30 GiB, gated, same HF_TOKEN as Alpha-Gen). No new node pack (same LTXVideo pin and nodes as Alpha-Gen).
2. **Graph:** one more LTX stage after the footage (LTX) and next to the Alpha-Gen stage, per shot with a subject layer: input = the shot clip (same `c<k>.clip` the matte chain `a.*` reads), output = plate clip. It is the `a.*` chain with a different `LTXICLoRALoaderModelOnly` file and the empty-scene prompt, so the cheapest build is to clone the `a.*` nodes per shot with `lora_name = clean plate`, `CLIPTextEncode.text = <empty-scene prompt>` and the SAME size rules as alpha (multiple of 32, single stage, 8k+1 frames, <= 145). Run it at 576x1024 (trained bucket).
3. **Prompt:** the planner already has the scene line and the subject (`concept.scene`, `concept.subject`). Template: `An empty clean plate of the exact same location: <scene without the subject>, as in the source video. No <subject>, no people, no figures, no body parts anywhere in the frame. No wake / reflection / cast shadow of <subject>. Static photorealistic footage, natural light, high detail.` The subject name must be in the positive text (README: name occluders explicitly); a design-LLM field or a template fill both work.
4. **Outputs / archive:** `MographArchive` needs a new per-shot ingredient `<stem>_sK_plate.mp4` (hardlinked into `ingredients/` like the clean / flow clips) + `tools/app/ingredients.py` manifest line; the clean clip (`_sK_clean.mp4`) and matte PNG stack already exist.
5. **Compose (`tools/fusion/compose.py` / the layered compositor, not touched here):** a shot with a plate gets TWO footage layers: plate (background, own camera move) and subject = clean x matte (own transform: drift / scale / rotation, tracked or keyframed), with the word / type layers between them. Needs: per-layer transform keyframes in the piece JSON, premultiplied subject + feathered matte edge + a colour match of the subject to the plate (section 3), an optional contact-shadow, and the matte at the same resolution as the clip (the app mattes are 1:1 with the clean clip). Plate pan must stay inside the plate: scale >= 1 + 2 x pan.
6. **Measure before shipping:** more subjects (people close-up, two subjects, fast action, subject filling > 50 % of the frame = out of scope per the LoRA card), cfg > 1 with the negative prompt, strength 1.0 vs 1.2, and the In-Outpainting mask route (section 1) as the fallback for stubborn residue.
7. **VRAM / order:** the stage peaks at ~23.5 GB on a 24 GB card: run it after the footage stage (never together; POST /free between), before compose. Cost: +33-49 s per 97 f shot on a 4090 (a 3-shot piece adds ~2-2.5 min at 576x1024).


## 6. App route: plate + independent subject layer + type as a matte (WIRED 2026-10-29, 24 GB tier; the box has NOT run it)
Code: `tools/fusion/plate_layers.py` (pure: shot keys, motion curves, colour fit, bake, letter reader, empty-scene prompt), graph stage `tools/app/layered_shots.py add_plate_chain`, nodes `MographPlatePrompt` / `MographPlateRef` / `MographLayerGather` (`nodes_fusion/layers.py`), compose hook `nodes_fusion/compose.py bake_plate_clips`, switch `llm_arms.TIERS[24].nodes` (`gather.plates` = true), tests `tests/test_plate_layers.py` (29).

**Shot keys** (piece JSON `shots[]`; all optional except the first): `"plate": "clean"`; `"subject_move": {"dx", "dy", "scale", "ease", "anchor", "feather"}` (END values from the identity at the first frame: dx / dy fractions of the frame, scale about `anchor` [0.5, 0.75], ease linear | ease_in | ease_out | ease_in_out, feather = matte softness as a fraction of the short side); `"plate_pan": {"dx", "dy", "scale", "ease"}` (the plate goes from +dx to -dx at a constant scale; the scale is raised to 1 + 2 max(|dx|, |dy|) so the pan stays inside the plate); `"plate_prompt"` (the whole Clean Plate prompt) / `"plate_subject"` (what to remove); `"type_matte"` (below). A bare `plate: "clean"` takes the probe numbers (subject +8 % sideways and 1.10, plate 1.12 and +-3.5 %). Who writes them: the concept `shot_plan` entry (optional keys already in the concept reply schema, `reply_schema.plate_angle_keys`); `plan_rules` has to copy `plate`, `subject_move`, `plate_pan`, `plate_subject`, `type_matte` from `sp_plan[k]` into `shots[k]` (one line, `plate_layers.norm_*` clamp them; NOT done: plan_rules / concept.py are another worker's files).

**Graph** (`*_layers*` graphs only; lazy, nothing runs unless a shot asks AND `gather.plates` is true): per slot `pl{k}.prompt` (MographPlatePrompt: the template of section 5.3 from the shot prompt) -> `pl{k}.6` / `pl{k}.8`, `pl{k}.ref` (MographPlateRef: the clean clip at the Alpha-Gen reference size, waits for every text pass), `p{k}.*` = a copy of the Alpha-Gen chain `a{k}.*` with the clean-plate LoRA (`pl.5`, `LTXICLoRALoaderModelOnly`), `p{k}.dec` (colour clip) -> `gather.plate_k`. A plate shot has NO text-rendered background (`LS.wants_text(plates=True)` is false): the type is flat between the layers. Alpha-Gen still cuts the matte from the CLEAN clip (`gather.alpha_ref_k`), so the matte and the subject are one picture. Order by data dependency: clean shots -> text passes -> plate stage -> Alpha-Gen -> depth -> design -> compose; the plate stage peaks near the card's 24 GB (section 3), so it has nothing else resident only in the sense that no other model is requested while it runs (Alpha-Gen needs its output; the design LLM and Video Depth Anything free ComfyUI's models first). Not measured in the app graph.

**Compose** (`bake_plate_clips`, before the recipe runs, so no recipe changes): footage = the plate moved by `plate_pan` (replicated border), subject = the clean clip colour-matched to the plate (gain / offset per channel fitted on the background pixels, matte dilated out; clamp 0.8-1.25) and moved by `subject_move`, matte = the Alpha-Gen matte mapped to the clip's geometry (`ref_to_clip`, the inverse of the cover crop), eroded and feathered, warped with the SAME matrix as the subject (`test_matte_follows_the_subject`: centroids agree within 0.6 px on every frame, also with scale and a matte clip of another length). Depth (if the recipe reads it) is warped with the plate's pan only. flow_wake reads the unmoved plate's flow (a slow pan is not in it). Not done: contact shadow / reflection for the moved subject, rotation, motion blur, the 3D pivot / orbit cut on a plate shot (the cut renders from the unbaked clips).

**Type as a matte** (`"type_matte": <mode>`, the user's request "also using the type as a matte as an option"; needs the plate, so it implies `plate: "clean"`; the letter matte is the type matte pass `<type_dir>/matte/matte_NNNNN.png`, frame `shot start + j`, gaussian softened 0.2 % of the short side; P = moved plate, S = moved subject, C = S over P, L = letters):
| mode | picture under the type | what stands in front of the type | when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reveal_subject` | P outside L, C inside L | nothing (the subject exists only inside the word) | the word is the reveal; the subject crosses behind it |
| `reveal_plate` | C outside L, P inside L | nothing (the word erases the subject) | a disappearance beat |
| `subject_through` | P | the subject, only where it overlaps L (matte x L) | the word is a window onto a fast, well-shaped subject |
| `plate_in_subject` | FLAT palette colour (accents[0]) -> the word (palette fg) -> the subject's matte silhouette filled with the CLEAN PLATE on top | nothing (one baked picture) | CONTRAST MOMENT: the empty scene shows through the person's shape, the word sits behind it |
| `plate_in_type` | FLAT colour -> the subject (clean clip x matte) -> the word on top, its letters FILLED WITH THE CLEAN PLATE | nothing (one baked picture) | CONTRAST MOMENT: the word is a window onto the empty scene, the man stands behind it on a flat ground |

## 7. Contrast moments (`plate_in_subject` / `plate_in_type`, 2026-10-30, own-video v003)
User ask: "use the matte to mask the clean plate into it and have the text in the background - also have the text matte filled with the clean plate and the matte of the main element behind it on a flat background - moments of visual contrast".
Code: `tools/fusion/plate_layers.py` `CONTRAST_MODES`, `contrast_picture(...)` (pure numpy: one picture from the moved plate / subject / matte / letters + a thin dark outline hugging the letters, never over the silhouette that stands in front of the word), `bake(..., flat_rgb, word_rgb)` (the two modes join `TYPE_MATTE_MODES`, so `shot_keys` / `type_matte_of` / `wants_plate` / the concept reply schema accept them), `bake_clip` passes the piece palette (accents[0] = flat ground, fg = word). The picture is baked into the same arrays as the reveal modes (`footage` = `subject` = the picture, `matte` empty), so the matte follows its own clip: with the identity move / pan the clip, its matte and the plate stay registered frame for frame (`test_matte_follows_the_subject_in_contrast_modes`).
Tests: `tests/test_plate_contrast.py` (6, pure numpy; run on the box: ComfyUI venv python, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""` while a render holds the GPU; pytest is not installed in the box venv, a 10-line runner calls the `test_*` functions).
They are MOMENTS, not a shot style: the own-video route (tools/app/own_video_v3.py `plan_moments` + `own_video_v3_moments.py`) draws 1-3 per variant from the words' holds, with an 8-frame iris in / out; the rest of the piece stays normal footage. **Occlusion guard + fill tint (2026-10-30, after the v003 preview3 QC: SECOND WORD under the singer was unreadable).** `plate_layers.bake` (so `bake_clip` and the own-video moment script) runs the guard before baking `window_subject_front` and `plate_in_type` (`OCC_GUARDED`): `occlusion_share` measures, over 12 sampled frames where the word is formed (letter area >= 60 % of its largest), the share of the WORD's area under the moved subject matte (> 0.5) and the worst single LETTER (connected blob); `occlusion_decision` compares them with `OCC_WORD = 0.35` / `OCC_LETTER = 0.70`. Over a limit: with `can_relayout` (the type stage can still redraw) -> `action: bigger_layout`, `layout: "grid_fill"` (letters filling the frame, extending past the subject), the mode is kept; otherwise (default) -> `action: swap`, mode `plate_in_subject`. The decision (action, mode, layout, measured shares, limits, reason) is returned in `info["occlusion"]` and `bake_clip` writes it into the piece (`plan.occlusion_guard.<shot>`); `guard=False` switches it off. `fill_tint` (default `FILL_TINT = 0.20`, shot key `fill_tint` 0..0.5, 0 = off) mixes that share of the word's ink into the plate / window fill so the fills sit in the piece palette; `flat: "dark" | "light"` is a shot key too (both accepted by `shot_keys` for the contrast modes and listed in `SHOT_KEYS`). Measured on v003 preview3 (real mattes + letter passes, no render): FIRST WORD 2.30-3.30 s covers 55 % (worst letter 100 %), SECOND WORD 5.30-7.30 s 71 %, LAST WORD 22.33-23.30 s 84 % -> all three would swap to `plate_in_subject`. Tests `tests/test_plate_contrast.py` (12 pass on the box, ComfyUI venv python, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`).

## 8. No flat fills inside the subject's silhouette (2026-10-30, user: "I dont want frames inside the silhouette to go flat colour")
- `plate_layers.fill_measure(frames, matte)` scores a candidate fill over a section INSIDE the matte: texture = mean local std (7x7 box, luma 0..255), motion = mean |luma diff| of frames 2 apart. `pick_fill` keeps the clean plate only above both floors (`FILL_MIN_TEXTURE` 5.0, `FILL_MIN_MOTION` 1.0), else the shot's own footage, else `other_clip` (the other shot's moving clip, `bake(other_clip=...)`), never a palette / flat fill; `fill_source` = plate | footage | other_clip forces one.
- Applies to the plate that fills the subject's silhouette (`plate_in_subject`). `bake` returns the choice + measurements in `info["fill"]`; `bake_clip` records `plan.fill_source.<shot>`; the moments splice (`own_video_v3_moments.py`) writes `fill_source`, `fill_measured`, `subject_strength` per moment in its report (moment key `fill_source` forces it).
- `subject_full_strength=True` (default): in the contrast modes the subject (window_subject_front: the skater's own matte-cut footage in front) is drawn untouched, the colour-fit gain / offset that matched it to the plate is not applied (no darkening / desaturation). `info["subject_strength"] = "full"`.
- Floors are provisional: measured on the v007 pottery plates (plate texture 10-12, motion 0.2-1.1; own footage texture 12-13, motion 1.5-3.8), not yet on the pale v026 plates. Tests: `tests/test_plate_contrast.py` (flat plate -> footage, textured plate kept, still footage + flat plate -> other_clip, forced, full strength): 20 pass on the box.
